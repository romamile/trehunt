MPI's treasure hunt, all content unlocked!

Thanks to everyone who played! We hope you had a lot of fun walking and exploring the MPI, guessing where the next QRcode would be and uncovering the truth. Around 80 people took part, about 20 finished the game in roughly 1h30, and an estimated 72,500 extra steps (maybe?) appeared on daily pedometers. If you’d like to revisit the whole experience, you can find the full content here, clues revealed and all stories accessible. But you still have to find who the murderer was!

Want to create your own treasure hunt? We plan to release soon our system open-source. We can also organise one for you. In both case, be sure to get in touch at roman.miletitch@gmail.com .
